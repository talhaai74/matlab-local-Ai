CHEATSHEET:
- Sampling: n points with spacing dt -> t = (0:n-1)*dt, fs = 1/dt, Nyquist = fs/2, lowest detectable frequency = 1/(n*dt),
  frequency resolution df = fs/n.
- DFT: Y = fft(y)/n (CE206 slides divide by n); Y(1) is the mean. Frequencies of Y(2:n/2+1) are (1:n/2)*fs/n.
  Slide plot: Y(1) = []; f = linspace(fmin, nyquist, n/2); stem(f, real(Y(1:n/2))) and stem(f, imag(Y(1:n/2))).
  Power spectrum: P = abs(Y(1:n/2)).^2 (after removing Y(1)); stem(f, P).
  ru_lib: F = fourier_dft(y) (= fft(y)); [f,P,A] = fourier_spectrum(y,dt) one-sided amplitude/power.
- Fourier series of a T-periodic function: [a0,a,b] = fourier_series(f,T,N); partial sum
  f_N(t) = a0 + sum(a(k)*cos(k*w0*t) + b(k)*sin(k*w0*t)), w0 = 2*pi/T. Square wave: sum of 4A0/((2n-1)pi)*sin(2pi(2n-1)t/T).
- Sinusoid fit y = A0 + A1*cos(w0 t) + B1*sin(w0 t): [A0,A1,B1,C1,theta] = fourier_sinefit(t,y,f0) (f0 = 1/T);
  amplitude C1 = sqrt(A1^2+B1^2), phase theta = atan2(-B1,A1), time of maximum t = -theta/w0 (mod T).
- Remove a trend before fft: y - polyval(polyfit(t,y,1),t). Cycle length = 1/(frequency of the largest power peak).
