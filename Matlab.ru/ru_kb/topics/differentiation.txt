METHODS: forward difference | backward difference | centered difference, central difference | richardson | gradient
CHEATSHEET:
- Function f: [d1,d2] = diff_fd(f, x, h, 'forward'|'backward'|'centered', order) (order 1/2 for forward/backward,
  2/4 for centered) gives f'(x) and f''(x). Richardson: D = diff_richardson(f, x, h1, h2) (= 4/3*D(h/2) - 1/3*D(h)).
  Formulas: centered O(h^2) f' = (f(x+h)-f(x-h))/(2h); forward O(h^2) f' = (-f(x+2h)+4f(x+h)-3f(x))/(2h).
- Data (x,y), equal or unequal spacing: [dy, d2y] = diff_data(x,y) (O(h^2) everywhere, one-sided at the ends).
  MATLAB built-ins: gradient(y,h) (equal spacing h, or gradient(y,x)), diff(y)./diff(x) (slopes between points,
  one fewer value; midpoints (x(1:end-1)+x(2:end))/2).
- Second derivative from first: apply diff_data/gradient twice, or use d2y from diff_data.
- Noisy data: fit a smooth curve (polyfit/spline) first, then differentiate the fit (polyder, fnder is a toolbox; use polyder).
- Inflection point: where d2y/dx2 changes sign (interpolate the zero); maximum slope where d2y/dx2 = 0.
- Flux: J = -k*dT/dx; shear stress tau = mu*du/dy at the wall; flow rate Q = dV/dt; area As = -dV/dz for volume
  measured from the surface down (use abs to report the area).
- True percent relative error vs the analytical derivative: et = abs((true - approx)/true)*100.
