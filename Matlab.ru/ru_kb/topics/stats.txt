CHEATSHEET:
- Base MATLAB: mean, median, mode, std (n-1), var, min, max, range = max-min, sort, cumsum, hist/histogram, corrcoef.
  mean(A) works column-wise; mean(A,2) row-wise. Coefficient of variation = std/mean*100 (%).
- Without the Statistics toolbox use ru_lib: stat_describe(x), stat_quantile(x,[0.25 0.5 0.75]) (quartiles like
  quantile/prctile), stat_ci_mean(x,0.95), [h,p,ci,st] = stat_ttest(x,mu0,alpha,tail), [h,p,ci,st] = stat_ttest2(x1,x2),
  stat_normcdf/stat_norminv/stat_normpdf, stat_tcdf/stat_tinv, s = stat_regress(X,y,intercept) (like fitlm).
- With the toolbox: quantile/prctile, iqr, ttest, ttest2, tinv, normcdf, fitlm(T,'y~x1+x2','Intercept',false).
- 95% CI of the mean: mean +/- tinv(0.975,n-1)*std/sqrt(n). IQR = Q3 - Q1.
- h = 1 means reject H0 at the alpha level (p < alpha). One-sided test "mean greater than mu0": tail 'right'.
- Data files: T = readtable('data_stat.xlsx'); x = T.PM25; the file must be in the current folder.
- Histogram with a range and width: edges = 0.8:0.2:2.4; histogram(x, edges) or counts = histc(x, edges).
